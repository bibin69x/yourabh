When someone in Kochi searches **"dentist near me"** or **"best bakery in Thrissur"**, Google shows a map with three businesses at the top. That spot, called the **Local Pack**, gets most of the clicks and calls. Here's how to get your business there.

## 1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single most important local SEO asset you have.

- Choose the most accurate **primary category** (e.g. "Dental clinic", not "Health")
- Add every relevant **secondary category**
- Write a clear description that includes your services and location
- Add accurate **opening hours**, including holiday hours for Onam, Vishu and Christmas
- Upload real photos every month: your shop, team, products and work

## 2. Keep your name, address and phone consistent everywhere

Google cross-checks your business details across the web. Make sure your **name, address and phone number (NAP)** are exactly the same on your website, Google profile, Facebook, Justdial, IndiaMART and any directories.

## 3. Get more (and better) Google reviews

Reviews are a major local ranking factor, and they're what convinces people to call you.

- Ask every happy customer, ideally with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp
- Reply to **every** review, good or bad, within 48 hours
- Never buy fake reviews. Google removes them and can suspend your profile

## 4. Build location pages on your website

If you serve several areas, such as Kochi, Aluva and Kakkanad, create a useful page for each one. Include real details: the services you offer there, directions, local projects and FAQs. Avoid copy-paste pages with only the city name changed.

## 5. Make your website fast and mobile-friendly

Most local searches happen on phones. A slow website loses customers before they even see your offer. Aim for a load time under 2.5 seconds and add a **click-to-call** and **WhatsApp** button on every page.

## 6. Post on your Google Business Profile weekly

Offers, new products, events and festival greetings all count. Regular posts show Google (and customers) that your business is active.

## The bottom line

Local SEO isn't a one-time setup. It's steady, consistent work, and businesses that keep at it can dominate their area for years.
