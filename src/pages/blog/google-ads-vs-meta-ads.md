It's one of the most common questions we hear: **"Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads?"** The honest answer is that it depends on how your customers buy. Here's a simple way to decide.

## The core difference

- **Google Ads captures demand.** People are already searching for what you sell, like "AC service in Kochi" or "visa consultants Dubai". You show up at the moment they need you.
- **Meta Ads creates demand.** People aren't searching. They're scrolling Facebook and Instagram. Great creative stops them and makes them want what you offer.

## Choose Google Ads first if…

- People actively search for your service (clinics, repairs, legal, education, B2B)
- You need leads **this week**
- Your customers have urgent needs
- Your average order value is high enough to justify higher click costs

## Choose Meta Ads first if…

- Your product is visual (fashion, food, real estate, interiors, beauty)
- You're launching something new that people don't know to search for yet
- You want WhatsApp conversations at a low cost
- You want to build a brand and retarget website visitors

## The best answer is usually both

Most growing businesses get the best results by combining the two:

1. **Meta Ads** build awareness and capture interest cheaply
2. **Google Ads** catch people when they're ready to buy
3. **Retargeting** on both platforms brings back visitors who didn't convert

## Don't skip tracking

Whichever platform you choose, set up proper conversion tracking for calls, form submissions and WhatsApp clicks. Without it, you're guessing, and guessing gets expensive.
